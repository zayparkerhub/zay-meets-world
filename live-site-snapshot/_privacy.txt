Privacy Notice

Zay Meets World · Last updated June 2026

What we collect

When you submit through this site we may collect your name, email address, phone number, social media handle, city, state, and any story or message you choose to share. We only collect what you choose to give us — nothing is required.

How we use it

Your information is used to reach out personally when we're heading to your city, to follow up on ideas and acts of love you've shared, and to build the community around this journey. We don't sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything unrelated to this project.

Who sees it

Submissions are stored securely and accessible only to the Zay Meets World team. We don't share individual submissions publicly without your permission.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing us. If you submitted anonymously and want to follow up, include any details from your submission so we can find it.
